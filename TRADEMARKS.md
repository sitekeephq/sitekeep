We want SiteKeep to be widely used and talked about. We also need people to know that a product called SiteKeep
really comes from the SiteKeep project. The rules below aim at both.

## You may, without asking

- **Use the name to refer to SiteKeep.** Write about it in articles, reviews, books, courses, presentations and
  blog posts; say that your product or service works with SiteKeep.
- **Distribute unmodified SiteKeep** under the name SiteKeep: the official releases, images and packages, or a
  build of an official release tag without changes to the code.
- **Name extensions and integrations "for SiteKeep"**, for example "Acme Scheduling for SiteKeep", as long as the
  name does not suggest that the extension is made or endorsed by the SiteKeep project.
- **Say where a fork comes from**: "based on SiteKeep" or "derived from SiteKeep", in plain text, in the
  description or the documentation of your own product.
- **Use the logo** to link to the SiteKeep project or to show that you support or use it, without changing it
  (see [the brand guide](docs/brand/README.md) for the files and how to use them).

## You need our written permission to

- **Distribute a modified version under the SiteKeep name or logo.** If you change the code in a way that
  requires you to share your source under the AGPL, your version is a fork: give it its own name and logo.
  Keep the AGPL notices and say where it comes from ("based on SiteKeep"), but do not call it SiteKeep.
- **Offer commercial services under the SiteKeep name**: hosting ("SiteKeep Cloud", "SiteKeep Hosting"),
  support, consulting, training or development advertised with the SiteKeep marks.
- **Use the marks in a company, product, domain or account name**: for example a domain or subdomain
  containing "sitekeep", an app-store listing, or a social media account that could be taken for an official
  one. Commercial hosting providers may not use "sitekeep" in their domain names.
- **Name a modified client, app or plug-in "SiteKeep …"**, such as "SiteKeep Pro" or "SiteKeep Mobile". A
  modified client must not carry "SiteKeep" in its name and must state clearly that it is not an official
  SiteKeep product.
- **Use the marks on merchandise** or in any way that suggests a partnership, certification or endorsement by
  7Dimensions OÜ.

## Never

- Use a name or logo confusingly similar to SiteKeep's for software, services or a company in the same field.
- Change the SiteKeep logo, combine it with your own marks, or use it in a misleading way.
- Register a trademark, company name or domain name that contains or imitates "SiteKeep".

## Contributions and forks

Contributing code to SiteKeep does not give the contributor any right to the SiteKeep marks
(see the [Contributor Licence Agreement](CLA.md)). A fork may keep the copyright notices and the AGPL licence,
and must replace the SiteKeep name and logos with its own.

Write to sitekeep@7dimensions.eu. We answer requests for partners, integrations, events and community use
gladly; most reasonable uses that help SiteKeep users are approved.
