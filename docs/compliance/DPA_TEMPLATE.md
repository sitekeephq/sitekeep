## 2. Instructions (Art. 28(3)(a))

The Processor processes the personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller,
including with regard to transfers to a third country, unless required to do so by Union or Member
State law; in that case it informs the Controller before processing, unless that law prohibits it.
The main agreement and this agreement are the Controller's instructions; further instructions are
given in writing (e-mail is sufficient) by `[authorised contacts]`. The Processor informs the
Controller immediately if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes data protection law.

## 3. Confidentiality (Art. 28(3)(b))

Persons authorised to process the personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are
under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

## 4. Security (Art. 28(3)(c), Art. 32)

The Processor takes the measures of Art. 32, at least: *access to the Controller's installation only
over encrypted connections and with named, two-factor protected accounts; access only for the
duration of a support case; backups encrypted at rest and in transit; logging of access; `[…]`*.
The measures are listed in Annex 2.

## 5. Sub-processors (Art. 28(2), (4))

The Processor engages another processor only with the Controller's `[prior specific / general]`
written authorisation. Authorised sub-processors at signature: Annex 3 (*e.g. the cloud provider where
backups or hosted installations are stored, with its location*). Under a general authorisation, the
Processor informs the Controller of intended changes `[30]` days in advance; the Controller may
object. The Processor imposes the same data protection obligations on each sub-processor by contract
and remains fully liable for it.

## 6. Assistance with data subject rights (Art. 28(3)(e))

Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor assists the Controller by
appropriate measures in answering requests of data subjects. Requests received directly are
forwarded to the Controller without undue delay. (SiteKeep's own tools for this are described in
[data subject rights](../features/DATA_SUBJECT_RIGHTS.md).)

## 7. Assistance with Art. 32–36 (Art. 28(3)(f))

The Processor assists the Controller with security, breach notification, impact assessments and
prior consultation. It notifies the Controller of a personal data breach **without undue delay** after
becoming aware of it (Art. 33(2)), *at the latest within `[24]` hours*, with the information of
Art. 33(3) as far as available.

## 8. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Personal data is transferred to a third country only on documented instructions and under Chapter V
GDPR. *Locations of processing: `[…]`*. Where the Controller is established in Türkiye, the transfer
to the Processor is governed by KVKK m.9 and the [KVKK standard contract](KVKK_STANDARD_CONTRACT_TEMPLATE.md).

## 9. End of the service (Art. 28(3)(g))

At the end of the service, the Processor, at the Controller's choice, deletes or returns all personal
data and deletes existing copies (including backups held by the Processor, within `[…]` days), unless
Union or Member State law requires storage. The Processor confirms the deletion in writing.

## 10. Audits and information (Art. 28(3)(h))

The Processor makes available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Art. 28 and
allows for and contributes to audits, including inspections, by the Controller or an auditor it
mandates, `[with reasonable notice, at most once a year unless after a breach]`.

## 11. Records

The Processor keeps a record of the processing carried out on behalf of the Controller (Art. 30(2)).
