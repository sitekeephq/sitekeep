# KVKK standard contract (controller → processor): template

> **DRAFT – for review by a lawyer, not legal advice.** This page is a preparation aid, not the
> contract. KVKK m.9 requires the **standard contract text announced by the Personal Data Protection
> Board**, used without changes; the Board's official text (controller to processor) must be taken
> from kvkk.gov.tr and completed with the details below. Have a lawyer review the completed contract
> before signing. Text in `[brackets]` is to be filled in.

When this is needed: a customer in Türkiye (the data controller, exporting party) has 7Dimensions OÜ
(Estonia, the processor, importing party) provide a service with access to the personal data held in
its SiteKeep installation: support or remote access, migration, backups in the 7Dimensions cloud, or
hosting outside Türkiye ([7Dimensions as processor](SUBPROCESSORS.md#7dimensions-as-processor)). Under
KVKK m.9 (as amended by Law No. 7499) and the Regulation on the Procedures and Principles for the
Transfer of Personal Data Abroad (Official Gazette 10.07.2024, No. 32598) this is a transfer abroad.

## Reminder: notification within five working days

> The signed standard contract must be **notified to the Personal Data Protection Authority within
> five working days of signing** (KVKK m.9(5)). Failing to notify is subject to an administrative
> fine (m.18(1)(d)). Record the date of signing and the date of notification.
